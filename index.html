Bruce

A strength workout tracker for iPhone. Pick a plan, log your sets, rest when the timer cues you, repeat.

Bruce keeps your plans and workout history on your device, with optional iCloud and Apple Health sync. It has no account, ads, subscription or analytics tracking, and it runs on iPhone with iOS 17.6 or later.
